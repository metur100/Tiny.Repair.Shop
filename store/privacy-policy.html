Privacy Policy

Tiny Repair Shop (Android and iOS) · App ID: com.certidevelopment.tinyrepairshop · Effective date: 10 October 2026

Short version: Tiny Repair Shop collects no personal data. The game makes no network requests, so nothing you do in it leaves your device. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no third-party services and no tracking.

1. Who is responsible

Tiny Repair Shop is developed and published by Medin Turkes.
Contact: certidevelopment@gmail.com

2. No personal data is collected

The game does not collect, process or transmit any personal data. It does not ask for your name, e-mail address or any other information about you, and it does not use any device or advertising identifiers. The customers in the game are fictional characters.

3. No network requests

Tiny Repair Shop works fully offline. The game sends no network requests: there is no server, no online account, no cloud save and no online leaderboard. The daily job is generated on your device from the date.

4. Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game uses local storage on your device only. This includes things like completed jobs and their best scores, coins, reputation and tickets, bought workshop upgrades and paint options, your favourite designs, daily job results, and your settings (for example sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and high contrast).

This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game. Depending on your device settings, your operating system may include app data in your own device backups (Google or iCloud backup). That is controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

5. No third parties, no tracking

  • No accounts, logins or sign-ups
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking, profiling or crash-reporting services
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No in-app purchases or payment data
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone

6. App stores

You download the game through Google Play or the Apple App Store. These platforms process data (for example your account and download information) under their own privacy policies, which we have no control over: Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy. If you opted in to share diagnostics with developers in your device settings, the platform may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the page and keep it secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. The cleaning demo runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing. If you e-mail us, we use your address and message only to answer you and delete them when no longer needed.

8. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed specifically at children. Because it collects no personal data at all, it also collects none from children. There are no ads, no purchases and no chat or social features.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the game does not send us any personal data, there is normally nothing we hold about you. Your game data stays under your control on your device. For questions, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
